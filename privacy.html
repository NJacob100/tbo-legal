Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 11, 2026

The Business Of ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mobile application that teaches users about how companies make money through interactive lessons and quizzes.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you create an account:

  • Email address — used for account creation and login
  • First name — used to personalise your experience
  • Age — used to ensure age-appropriate content

We also collect the following usage data:

  • Quiz responses — to track your learning progress
  • Streak and completion data — to display your stats
  • Subscription status — managed by RevenueCat to process payments

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalise the app experience
  • To track your learning progress and streaks
  • To process subscription payments via Apple's App Store
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app stability (via Sentry)

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — for secure authentication and data storage
  • RevenueCat — for subscription management and payment processing
  • Sentry — for crash reporting and error monitoring

We do not sell, share, or provide your personal data to third parties for advertising purposes.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase with row-level security policies. Authentication tokens are stored in secure device storage. All data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete your account at any time from the Settings screen within the app. This permanently removes all your data.
  • Cancel your subscription at any time through your device's Settings app.

Children's Privacy

The app is designed for educational purposes. We require users to provide their age during sign-up. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without parental consent.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the app.
