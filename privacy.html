Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 29, 2026

The Business Of ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mobile application that teaches users about how real companies make money through interactive breakdowns of business models, financials, and investment cases.

Information We Collect

The Business Of is designed to be privacy-friendly. We do not require account creation or login. We collect the following data:

  • Device identifier — we generate an anonymous device token (your device's push notification token or a locally generated ID) to associate your data with your device. This is not linked to your name, email, or Apple ID.
  • Reading progress — which company breakdowns you have opened and completed. This is stored on your device and backed up to our servers so your progress can be recovered if you change devices.
  • Investment ideas — if you use the Ideas feature, your investment theses, notes, bear cases, and other text you enter are stored on your device and backed up to our servers. These are associated with your anonymous device token, not your identity.
  • Pitch requests — if you request a stock pitch or upload a pitch PDF for review, the company name, your optional backup email, and the uploaded file are stored on our servers.
  • Subscription status — if you subscribe, your purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID.

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number (unless you voluntarily provide an email as a backup on a pitch request).
  • We do not require account creation or login.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the app:

  • Supabase — for secure cloud storage of your reading progress, investment ideas, and pitch requests. Data is stored in the EU (London region). Supabase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — for subscription management. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store — for payment processing. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Yahoo Finance — for stock price data. When you view price charts or company metrics, your device makes requests to Yahoo Finance's public API containing stock ticker symbols. No personal data is sent. Yahoo Privacy Policy
  • Mux — for video streaming of career advice content. Mux Privacy Policy

We do not sell, share, or provide your personal data to third parties for advertising purposes.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in two places:

  • On your device — reading progress, ideas, and preferences are stored locally for fast access and offline use.
  • On our servers — a backup copy of your reading progress and ideas is stored on Supabase (hosted in the EU) so your data can be recovered if you change devices. Pitch requests and uploaded PDFs are also stored on our servers.

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

Your Rights

Under GDPR and UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access your data — request a copy of all data we hold associated with your device. Contact us at the email below and we will provide your data within 30 days.
  • Delete your data — request deletion of all data associated with your device. You can do this by contacting us at the email below, or by using the "Delete my data" option within the app. We will delete your data from our servers within 30 days.
  • Data portability — request your data in a machine-readable format.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as you use the app. If you delete the app or request deletion of your data, we will remove all associated data from our servers within 30 days. Anonymised, aggregated usage statistics (such as total number of breakdowns read across all users) may be retained indefinitely.

Children's Privacy

The app is designed for educational purposes and is suitable for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since the app does not require account creation and collects only anonymous device identifiers, no parental consent mechanism is needed for users aged 13 and over.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page. If we make significant changes to how we handle your data, we will notify you through the app.
